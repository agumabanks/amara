# Walkthroughs

## Ask about bookings

1. Owner asks, “Do I have any available bookings in Soko Terminal?”
2. Amara identifies the trusted read-only booking skill.
3. Amara checks Accessibility and launches Terminal.
4. She distinguishes account sign-in, staff PIN, POS home, and unexpected states.
5. From POS, she opens Alerts → Needs action → Bookings.
6. She waits for cards to settle, reads all visible pages, and deduplicates them.
7. She reports exact service/customer pairs and available actions without changing anything.
8. If authentication is required, she names the exact screen and owner action instead.

## Audit and polish a service

1. Owner asks which services need polishing.
2. Amara scans all service cards and records coverage.
3. She opens candidate details and captures text plus visual evidence.
4. She produces structured findings and proposed before/after changes.
5. Owner follows up, “Polish the first one and save it.”
6. Amara resolves that phrase to the exact pending finding, shows the final diff, and requests any required approval.
7. After approval, she edits, saves, reopens, and verifies.
8. She reports the persisted fields and evidence.

## Scheduled Soko advertisement to WhatsApp

1. Owner defines target, frequency, quiet hours, and approval policy.
2. Amara stores a durable schedule and idempotency key.
3. At execution, she checks user activity, battery/network constraints, and existing occurrence receipt.
4. She opens Studio, selects an eligible non-repeated ad, and verifies product facts.
5. She drafts or polishes the caption without inventing facts.
6. She sends only to the approved target and verifies the correct chat and message/media.
7. She records delivery state and reports the occurrence once.

## WhatsApp inbound reply

1. Notification access identifies sender, conversation, message, and group state.
2. Amara applies deduplication and monitoring policy.
3. She opens the exact chat, reads visible context, and combines only relevant Soko memory.
4. She classifies the message and either drafts, replies under policy, or escalates.
5. She verifies the sent content in the correct chat and records follow-up timing.

## TikTok post

1. Owner requests or approves an exact product and media asset.
2. Amara opens TikTok Create, selects the verified asset, and creates a grounded caption.
3. Draft is the default unless publish approval or standing policy exists.
4. On publish, Amara verifies the resulting post/profile state and stores a duplicate-prevention receipt.
