# What makes 8/10 defensible

**These are proposed acceptance rules, not achieved results or owner-ratified targets.** Ratify them and freeze the scope before a run. “8/10” is a bounded product acceptance decision for Sanaa Media, not a general intelligence score.

## Decision rule

Accept only when all of the following hold:

1. At least 80 of 100 weighted points are accepted using the criteria in `scorecard.json`.
2. Every critical gate passes; a strong TikTok score cannot offset wrong-recipient messages or absent commercial proof.
3. One unchanged acceptance scope completes at least seven consecutive days with the required traffic and no routine owner rescue. Record build/settings changes and restart the affected proof window rather than combining incompatible results.
4. The owner reviews evidence, confirms economic value and signs the defence record.

A gate earns its full weight only after its complete criteria are met. PARTIAL, FAIL and UNPROVEN earn no accepted points. This conservative evidence coverage is **not a capability percentage**. Test data may cover difficult cases, but must be labelled and cannot count as real demand, revenue or unassisted customer traffic.

## Proposed gates

| ID | Weight | Critical | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---:|:---:|---|
| G1 Identity and truthful commitments | 15 | Yes | Zero wrong-recipient sends, cross-customer disclosure or unauthorised price/delivery promises in the run; all held ambiguity cases visible; controlled direct/group/same-name cases covered |
| G2 Useful, timely customer attention | 20 | Yes | ≥95% of eligible conversation turns get a useful verified response within 120 seconds; ≥50 turns across ≥10 chats in ≥7 days; review every pilot response for factual accuracy and next step |
| G3 Follow-through and handoff | 15 | Yes | ≥95% of ≥20 due follow-ups/handoffs completed on time; stop/decline honoured; owner receives need, context and requested decision |
| G4 Group participation | 10 | No | ≥20 due authorised photo promotions across ≥3 individually identified groups; ≥95% verified within agreed window or explicitly excluded before becoming due; relevance review; no repeated unresolved dispatch or ignored opt-out |
| G5 TikTok audience value | 10 | No | ≥20 planned posts across ≥7 days; ≥95% verified within agreed schedule; review content against editorial brief; ≥10 authorised interactions verified; record real audience/enquiry baseline and change |
| G6 Soko buying clarity | 10 | Yes | ≥5 real owner-approved offerings have complete factual buying information; ≥3 listing improvements with verified before/after evidence; at least one listing-to-enquiry-to-order record |
| G7 Learning used in decisions | 5 | No | ≥3 reviewed feedback-to-change-to-outcome chains; one measured positive business/customer result; regressions and rejected lessons recorded |
| G8 Owner independence and reporting | 5 | Yes | Seven days without routine rescue; seven truthful daily reports; review unresolved work and measurable saved time; interruptions recorded |
| G9 Commercial value | 10 | Yes | Owner-defined weekly paid-order/contribution or saved-time target met; ≥3 attributed genuine paid orders; costs and refunds reconciled; no double-counted assisted revenue |

If traffic is too low, extend the run. Do not reduce the denominator, generate fake enquiries or declare a quiet week a pass. Three paid orders are minimum traceability evidence, not proof of broad product-market fit or statistically established uplift.

## Metric definitions

- **Eligible conversation turn:** a new actionable customer request in an authorised conversation. Group bursts may be combined before response; preserve constituent events and the earliest unanswered actionable timestamp. Exclude transport notifications/spam using recorded reasons. Do not exclude difficult conversations after failure.
- **Useful response:** answers the question accurately or requests needed information, gives a sensible next step, and avoids unsupported commitments. A generic greeting is not enough.
- **Response time:** earliest eligible notification observation to verified send. This is observed phone latency, not guaranteed customer-send latency; separately disclose capture gaps and outages.
- **Follow-up denominator:** all agreed follow-ups/handoffs due within the window, including failed attempts. Mark cancellation/decline explicitly before removing eligibility.
- **Publication denominator:** all scheduled, authorised, due opportunities in the frozen plan. A pause after failure is a reported failure/disposition, not a retroactive exclusion.
- **Verified publication:** target-bound evidence of content and sent/published state. Does not mean viewed, read, welcomed or converted.
- **Qualified enquiry:** identifiable interest in a specific service/product with sufficient need information for the next sales step; never count a generic emoji as demand.
- **Attributed order:** unique order ID linked to offering and source/enquiry, payment evidence and owner reconciliation. Distinguish direct, assisted and unknown attribution.
- **Contribution:** collected revenue minus refunds, fulfilment costs and agreed Amara operating costs. Record currency; do not mix generic code scoring units with actual UGX accounting.
- **Rescue:** owner/developer intervenes to complete or unblock routine work. Normal quote approval is a designed handoff, not rescue. Count these separately; unknown stays unknown.

## Hard stops for a passing claim

Any wrong-recipient send, cross-customer disclosure, ignored revocation, fabricated delivery/revenue claim or concealed unresolved dispatch prevents acceptance pending remediation and new scoped evidence. An isolated safely held failure need not invalidate all work, but it remains in the metrics.

## Defence meeting

Show the build/settings manifest, denominators, exclusions, transaction evidence, customer-quality review, cost/order ledger and intervention log. Demonstrate one complete real sales journey and one safely handled failure. Use [the defence template](templates/DEFENCE-REVIEW.md). The owner can accept the defined pilot scope, request another run, or reject it. Do not substitute a polished dashboard for evidence.
