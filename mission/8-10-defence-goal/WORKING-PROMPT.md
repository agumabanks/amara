# Improved working prompt

> Work toward a defensible 8/10 Amara for Sanaa Media: a phone-based sales assistant that provides accurate customer attention, relevant marketing, reliable follow-up and traceable business value with little routine supervision.
>
> Start with this mission pack, current code and the latest available device evidence. Preserve the distinction between proposed, implemented, tested, deployed, observed and accepted. Prioritise the highest-impact gap in the customer-to-order journey, not activity volume or feature count.
>
> For each task: identify the customer problem; cite the evidence; define the desired behaviour and acceptance criterion; implement the authorised change; run proportionate checks; record build/deployment identity when applicable; then collect honest live evidence. Keep unresolved sends visible and do not blindly repeat them. Maintain correct recipient identity, approved business facts, group permissions and owner authority.
>
> Update tasks, proposals, the implementation map, evidence index, execution log and scorecard. Regenerate the progress dashboard. Never count tests or deployment as proof of sales, customer usefulness or unattended reliability. Do not award an 8/10 until the defence rules pass and the owner reviews them.
>
> Use market and conversation learning to make a specific, reviewable improvement to an offering, reply, follow-up or content decision. Measure its effect. Keep raw customer content and secrets out of mission reports. Separate real demand from controlled tests, verified outcomes from attempts, and revenue from profit.
>
> Proceed autonomously within existing authorisation. Ask only for missing business decisions that affect correct customer promises or acceptance; continue independent work while awaiting them. Report what changed, evidence, remaining limitations and the next highest-value step.

## Reuse boundaries

This prompt is a maintained instruction artifact. Creating it does not itself start every roadmap task, change production settings, publish content, open a new evaluation window or ratify proposed business targets. Apply the active user's scope and existing authorisations each time.
