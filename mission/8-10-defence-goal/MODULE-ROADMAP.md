# Product roadmap

Sequence is evidence-driven. Dates depend on traffic, owner decisions and verification; no delivery estimates have been invented.

| Phase | Deliverable | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| 0 — Define the job | One Sanaa Media service, approved facts, audience and weekly value target | Owner decisions recorded; acceptance plan frozen |
| 1 — Care for the customer | Accurate response, identity continuity, qualification and clear handoff | G1/G2 demonstrated; unresolved historical cases reviewed without blind resend |
| 2 — Complete the sale | Follow-up, Soko clarity, payment/order outcome and reporting | G3/G6/G9 evidence exists |
| 3 — Earn attention | Group-specific participation and useful TikTok series | G4/G5 demonstrated without degrading inbound care |
| 4 — Improve independently | Learning decisions and an uninterrupted working week | G7/G8; owner defence review |

## WhatsApp direct

Current: identity routing and review queue exist. Next: customer journey from first question to quote-ready brief; recognise returning customers through verified identity; follow-up timing suited to the conversation; clear escalation when a promise needs the owner. Close with a next step, not repeated generic sales language. Prove short names, same names, phone-number labels, groups, missed calls and route loss. Historical unresolved sends require read-only reconciliation before any resend decision.

## WhatsApp groups

Current: listening/reply/promotion controls, photos, intervals and failure holds. Next: a group profile containing purpose, allowed content, posting rules, useful categories, tone, cadence and owner notes. Distinguish relevant buying requests from general chatter. Let listening inform offer selection without moving private group/member content into public ads. Give the owner an explicit way to reconcile duplicate saved/origin entries; never auto-merge by name. Demonstrate that destination recovery works before raising volume. Measure relevant enquiries and negative feedback, not ad count alone.

## TikTok

Current: publication and social routines; verified posts but uncertain comments. Next: approved editorial brief with three or four repeatable series: finished work, buying advice, behind the scenes and specific offers. Establish a consistent Sanaa Media identity, opening hook and clear enquiry route. Sample comments for usefulness; use genuine interaction rather than mass following or generic emoji activity. Compare suitable posting frequencies using content quality, qualified enquiries and customer-care performance. The existing 30-minute setting is owner-selected operational configuration, not proven optimal marketing strategy.

## Soko

Current: access and catalogue/intelligence workflows. Next: complete each service listing with deliverable, inclusions, options, indicative versus fixed price, turnaround, required customer inputs, examples and contact/order route. Competitor observations should suggest factual improvements; they must not become unsupported price/stock promises or copied product claims. Preserve before/after listing evidence and owner approval for commercial changes.

## Market intelligence and learning

Ask one decision question before browsing Jiji/Jumia: which customer uncertainty, offer weakness or opportunity are we investigating? Save source/date, observation, uncertainty and proposed action. Approve the lesson, apply a bounded change, compare outcomes and retain/revert. Research completion is not a learning success metric. Keep phone/backend knowledge permissions and retention explicit; check actual sync before claiming both copies exist.

## Owner experience

One daily report: business gained, customer needs, due follow-ups, unresolved actions, top lesson, proposed change and one concise owner decision. The work dashboard must reveal stalled opportunities, not just an empty queue. A business setup brief should make approved facts and decision authority easy to maintain without exposing implementation detail.

## Expansion after acceptance

Test a second Sanaa Media service, then a distinct business. Revalidate product assumptions and service/retail differences. A device demonstration is not evidence of reliable onboarding on every phone model.
